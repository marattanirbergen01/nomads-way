## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — under a wide cool sky, soft volumetric light, wide cinematic composition with a low horizon. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts with wooden shanyrak crowns, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. A long migration column crossing snow-dusted winter steppe. Loaded camels, riders wrapped in sheepskin, sledges and driven herds stretch in a thin line from the foreground to the pale horizon. Low orange sun, long blue shadows, breath steaming from animals. Wind-scoured drifts and dry grass poking through snow. Endurance rather than misery; the line does not end within the frame.
