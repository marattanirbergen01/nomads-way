## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — under a wide cool sky, soft volumetric light, wide cinematic composition with a low horizon. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts with wooden shanyrak crowns, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. A nomad camp breaking up at sunrise. Yurt felts rolled and lashed onto kneeling Bactrian camels, dismantled lattice walls stacked, dogs and children among the bundles. In the middle ground a sultan in a dark chapan stands on a low rise speaking to a gathered crowd of several dozen people, his arm raised; the crowd listens, some already mounted. On the far left a small ancestral burial ground with weathered stone and clay markers. Movement, purpose and quiet grief at once.
