## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Shaybanid court in Mawarannahr. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes.  No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. 1509, evening inside a rich campaign tent of patterned silk and felt, lit by a glowing brazier and by two small copper bowls of oil, each with a single burning wick, hanging on chains; layered carpets on the ground, the tent walls hung with woven rugs and cloth. In the upper third the dark curve of the tent roof with the two hanging copper bowls of burning oil, their small flames flickering. In the middle of the frame, the two of them alone in a quiet tent whose floor and walls hold only carpets, cushions, rugs and the brazier, on the left, an elderly Persian scholar with a long greying beard, a large white turban and a dark brown robe over a cream undergarment with a cream sash sits cross-legged at a low wooden desk with an open bound notebook, a thin reed pen with a cut tip raised in his hand, a small brass inkwell beside the notebook. On the right, facing him across the glowing brazier, a slender young prince with a thin black moustache, a neat white turban with a small red cap and a robe of deep green silk with a gold pattern at the collar sits on cushions with his eyes lowered to the flames of the brazier, thoughtful, his head slightly bowed. Warm firelight, deep shadows, intimate and quiet. The patterned carpets run out of the bottom of the frame.
