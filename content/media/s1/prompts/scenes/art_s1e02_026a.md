# art_s1e02_026a — Ребёнок спасён

Сцена `sc026a` серии s1e02, **личный слой аватара**. В кадре / в кружках: Айдар (бюст), Бекет, Темир.

Модель: **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B 8-bit** (Draw Things). Аватар в кадре не рисуется: плеер кладёт его бюст слева
поверх сцены (`AvatarBust`), поэтому левая часть средней трети остаётся спокойной.

## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome. This is what the player sees with their own eyes: the player's own figure is NOT in the picture, and the left side of the middle third is kept quiet and dark enough for a portrait to be placed over it. Night in a narrow alley of Syghnaq between blank mud-brick walls. In the middle of the frame, close to the viewer, a small boy of about seven in a little quilted coat and a felt cap presses himself against the wall, frightened, looking up at the viewer with wide eyes. Far down the alley, in the upper middle of the frame, the orange glow of torches as riders pass across its end. In the upper third a strip of black sky between the walls. Cold darkness, silence, held breath.
