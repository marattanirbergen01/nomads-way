## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — under a wide cool sky, soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts as low round tents of white felt: a straight drum wall and a smooth shallow domed roof closing at the very top in a small flat wooden ring that lies flush with the felt, the roofline clean and unbroken, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. A nomad camp breaking up at sunrise, seen from inside the crowd looking up at the two sultans. In the upper middle of the frame, on a low grassy rise, stand the two sultans side by side, facing the crowd, both seen from the front. On the left Kerei, the elder: about fifty, a dark moustache and a short dark goatee, two thin braids beside his cheeks, a tall börik with a wide band of pale fur and a red-and-gold striped crown topped with a white plume, a deep blue chapan with gold brocade at the collar; his right arm is raised as he speaks. On the right Zhanibek, the younger: about forty, a dark moustache and a small pointed black goatee, a rounded cap with a cream crown and a deep blue band topped with a white plume, a pale gold chapan with a curling ornament, a knife at his belt; he stands calm with a hand on his belt. Below them, filling the middle and lower frame, a crowd of several dozen people seen mostly from behind and in profile as they look up at the sultans — men in quilted chapans and fur hats, women in headscarves, a few already mounted on horses at the edges; all with the faces of the steppe: broad cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches, bare chins. Beside the crowd a kneeling Bactrian camel loaded with rolled felts, dismantled lattice walls stacked on the ground. On the far left a small ancestral burial ground with weathered stone markers. Movement, purpose and quiet grief at once.
