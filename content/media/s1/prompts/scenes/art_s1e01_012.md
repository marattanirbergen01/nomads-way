## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. All the people are Kazakh nomads with the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin dark moustaches and bare chins, at most a short sparse goatee on the elders. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — under a wide cool sky, soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts as low round tents of white felt: a straight drum wall and a smooth shallow domed roof closing at the very top in a small flat wooden ring that lies flush with the felt, the roofline clean and unbroken, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. Ravaged nomad camp in the morning after a raid. Three mounted tax collectors in fine quilted robes and fur hats sit their horses in front of a small group of people on foot; one villager holds the halter of the last remaining horse while a collector reaches for the rope. Empty wooden horse pen behind them, a half-collapsed yurt frame, scattered household goods. Tense, wordless standoff — no weapons drawn, no violence. Cold flat morning light, figures seen from a respectful middle distance, faces small and turned away.
