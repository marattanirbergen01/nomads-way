## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes. All the people are Kazakh nomads with the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches and bare chins. Felt yurts as low round tents of white felt: a straight drum wall and a smooth shallow domed roof closing at the very top in a small flat wooden ring that lies flush with the felt, the roofline clean and unbroken, the air above every roof clear and still. Short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. 1468, evening on the steppe, seen from a height. In the upper third the horizon glowing orange after sunset under a darkening sky. In the middle third a large encampment of many yurts with dozens of campfires glowing among them, and from three directions caravans of camels and groups of riders with banners on lances converging on it, their dust catching the last light. Small figures everywhere, no close faces. Epic scale, a people gathering.
