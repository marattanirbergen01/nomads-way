## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome crowned by a small gilded crescent; every tower top is flat with battlements. This is what the player sees with their own eyes: the player's own figure is NOT in the picture, and the left side of the middle third is kept quiet and dark enough for a portrait to be placed over it. Night. A storeroom behind the bazaar seen from outside in the dark, through a small open window in a mud-brick wall that frames the scene. Inside, in warm oil-lamp light, a stout elderly Sart merchant with a short grey beard, a low white turban and a robe of dark red and mustard yellow stripes and a hard-faced rider of Shaibani's men in dark mail, a pointed helmet wrapped with a band of white cloth clasp hands over a low table; on the table lie a small pile of silver coins and an open bound account book. Sacks and bales stacked in shadow around them. The dark wall around the window fills the edges of the frame. Secrecy, betrayal overheard.
