# art_s1e02_045 — Допрос есаула

Сцена `sc045 и sc046` серии s1e02, **личный слой аватара**. В кадре / в кружках: есаул, Темир, Сейит, Дана, Айдар (бюст).

Модель: **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B 8-bit** (Draw Things). Аватар в кадре не рисуется: плеер кладёт его бюст слева
поверх сцены (`AvatarBust`), поэтому левая часть средней трети остаётся спокойной.

No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome crowned by a small gilded crescent; every tower top is flat with battlements. This is what the player sees with their own eyes: the player's own figure is NOT in the picture, and the left side of the middle third is kept quiet and dark enough for a portrait to be placed over it.
