## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome crowned by a small gilded crescent; every tower top is flat with battlements. Deep dusk at the walls of Syghnaq during a siege, seen from the ground at the foot of the wall, looking up. In the lower part of the frame, in the foreground, a line of Kazakh archers seen from behind in quilted coats and fur hats, drawing their bows; the arrows in their hands and quivers have red-painted shafts with a twisted pattern and a black band just behind the iron head, clearly visible. In the middle third the tall dust-coloured town wall, with a long crack running down it beside a gate on the left. A tall square tower rises from the wall into the upper third, its flat top edged with battlements. Standing behind those battlements on the roof of the tower, so that only her head, shoulders and raised arm show above the parapet, a small girl with two long black braids and a small dark red cap holds up an oil lamp and swings it — a single bright spark of warm light at the top of the tower against the deep blue evening sky. Above the tower stretches a clear, deep blue evening sky. Arrows in flight, dust in the air, a few torches on the wall.
