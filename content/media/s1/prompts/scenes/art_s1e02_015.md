# art_s1e02_015 — Предложение Сейита

Сцена `sc015` серии s1e02, **личный слой аватара**. В кадре / в кружках: Сейит, Дана, Айдар (бюст).

Модель: **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B 8-bit** (Draw Things). Аватар в кадре не рисуется: плеер кладёт его бюст слева
поверх сцены (`AvatarBust`), поэтому левая часть средней трети остаётся спокойной.

## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome. This is what the player sees with their own eyes: the player's own figure is NOT in the picture, and the left side of the middle third is kept quiet and dark enough for a portrait to be placed over it. Early 1470s, evening in the bazaar of Syghnaq as it closes. In the upper third the dust-coloured town wall and the turquoise dome of the mosque against a dusky violet sky. The stalls are half packed up, cloth taken down, sacks tied shut, a few oil lamps lit. In the middle of the frame, close to the viewer and turned towards them, a stout elderly Sart merchant with a short grey beard, a low white turban and a robe of dark red and mustard yellow stripes holds out a small heavy leather pouch of silver towards the viewer in his open hand, a sly half-smile. Behind him on the right, in the warm light of a hanging lamp, a girl of sixteen with two long black braids, a small dark red velvet cap with a tuft of white owl feathers, a dark blue sleeveless kamzol over a long white dress stands holding a bundle of arrows and watches with suspicion. The empty market floor runs out of the bottom of the frame.

## Замечание по кадру
Тот же базар, что art_s1e02_010, но вечер. Делать **img2img от утверждённого 010 на его seed, strength 1.0**,
чтобы место совпало (так делали пару 032_river → 045_river в s1e01). Сейит протягивает мешочек «в камеру» — зрителю,
то есть аватару; самого аватара в кадре нет, его бюст кладёт плеер.

- Size: 768×1344 (9:16, вертикально) · Steps: 4 · Guidance: 1.0 · Sampler: Euler A Trailing · Shift: 3.0 · Negative: пусто
- Окно текста закрывает нижнюю треть экрана: смысл кадра — в верхних двух третях, внизу земля.
- Seed записать в manifest.yaml после утверждения кадра.
