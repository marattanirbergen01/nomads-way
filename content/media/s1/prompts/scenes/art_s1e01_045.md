## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the 15th-century Kazakh steppe. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — under a wide cool sky, soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts as low round tents of white felt: a straight drum wall and a smooth shallow domed roof closing at the very top in a small flat wooden ring that lies flush with the felt, the roofline clean and unbroken, the air above every roof clear and still, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. Blue hour on a low ridge above a valley. The bare grassy crest of a low hill fills the foreground, dry grass and a few rocks, empty ground with no one standing on it. Far below, small in the distance, the yurts and scattered fires of a large camp glow across the darkening plain, horses at the edge of the firelight. The whole picture is landscape only: no people anywhere in the frame, the hilltop left empty for the player's figure to be placed over it. Vast quiet sky with the first stars. Contemplative closing shot, no action.
