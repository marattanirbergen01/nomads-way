## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. Sunset on top of the town wall of Syghnaq, the day after the battle. In the middle of the frame two men stand side by side at the parapet, seen from behind, looking out over the land: on the left Burundyq — a man of about forty with a very wide, broad, full face, smooth unlined skin, a thin black moustache and a small pointed black goatee, cheeks clean-shaven, black hair; he wears a tall cap of faded dusty-rose brocade that widens towards the top, with large orange-and-gold swirling ornament and a green-and-gold border, and two long brocade flaps hanging down beside his face to his chest; a dark red kaftan with a high standing collar and a dense small woven pattern, his right forearm bound in a linen bandage; on the right Qasym — a tall lean young man with long black hair to his shoulders and a short black beard, a pointed steel helmet with a gilded band and a thin spike, a grey wolf-fur cloak over a dark green chapan. A little apart on the right, a girl with two long black braids and a small dark red cap leans on the parapet. Beyond them, filling the upper half, the Syr Darya winds wide and shining through a flat plain of reeds and fields towards the horizon, the low sun large and orange above the water. The dusty top of the wall runs out of the bottom of the frame. Quiet, thoughtful, one peaceful evening on a contested border.
