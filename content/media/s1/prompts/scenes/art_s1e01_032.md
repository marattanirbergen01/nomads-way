Period-accurate nomadic dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed malakhai hats, felt boots, woven belts. Felt yurts with wooden shanyrak crowns, short sturdy steppe horses, Bactrian camels. A settled, breathing camp rather than a roadside halt. Warm firelight against cool blue dusk.
