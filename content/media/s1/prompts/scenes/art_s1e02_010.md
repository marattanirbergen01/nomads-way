## Prompt
A painterly semi-realistic historical illustration of the late 15th-century Kazakh steppe and the towns of the Syr Darya. Muted earth palette — ochre, dust, faded indigo, weathered leather brown — soft volumetric light, a tall vertical composition for a phone screen: sky in the upper third, the action in the middle third, the ground running out of the bottom of the frame. Period-accurate dress: quilted chapan robes, fur-trimmed hats, felt boots, woven sashes; Kazakh warriors in quilted coats and light mail, pointed steel helmets or fur hats, bows and lances. All the steppe people have the faces of the steppe: broad flat cheekbones, narrow dark eyes, thin moustaches. Short sturdy steppe horses. No plate armour, no fantasy elements, no modern objects, no lettering anywhere in the image. The town of Syghnaq: thick walls of rammed earth and sun-dried brick the colour of dust, squat square towers, flat roofs, and one fired-brick mosque with a modest turquoise-tiled dome crowned by a small gilded crescent; every tower top is flat with battlements. Early 1470s, a bright clear morning in the bazaar of Syghnaq on the middle Syr Darya. In the upper third the dust-coloured town wall with a square tower and, rising behind it, the turquoise dome of the mosque against a pale sky. In the middle third a crowded open market: sacks of wheat with their mouths rolled open, bolts of dyed cloth hanging from poles, copper cauldrons stacked on a mat, grey blocks of rock salt, and on the right steppe herders in fur hats driving a flock of sheep in through the open gate. In the middle of the frame, at a stall, two people seen from the side and in profile, mid-sized, turned towards each other: on the left a stout elderly Sart merchant with a short grey beard, a low white turban and a robe of dark red and mustard yellow stripes, holding up a balance scale; on the right a girl of sixteen with two long black braids, a small dark red velvet cap with a tuft of white owl feathers, a dark blue sleeveless kamzol over a long white dress, holding a bundle of arrows against her chest. Busy, prosperous, peaceful.
